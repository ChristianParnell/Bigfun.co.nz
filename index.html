A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
press start.

How much does photobooth hire cost?

We quote for your event, chosen booth, hire duration and location. Send your details and we’ll confirm pricing and availability.

Can I get a photobooth permanently placed?

Yes. Tell us about your venue, available space and proposed arrangement. BigFun maintains permanent placements; the service schedule and commercial terms are agreed with each venue.

Can the photos match my wedding or brand?

Tell us your design ideas in the enquiry form. We’ll discuss available photo-strip, card and branding options for the selected booth.

What space or power does a booth need?

Requirements depend on the booth model and site. Include approximate space, power availability and any stairs or access restrictions. We’ll confirm the setup requirements before booking.

Is sending the form a confirmed booking?

No. It’s a request for availability or a placement discussion. A booking is confirmed only after the BigFun team agrees the details with you.
